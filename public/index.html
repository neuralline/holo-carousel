H.O.L.O carousel

Holo 3.1 on Cyre v5 - useHolo(), built from markup or data. Swipe, drag, use the arrow keys or the wheel.

From markup: holo.scan()

Scanning is opt-in. These two carousels are plain HTML; options come from their data-* attributes.

From data, with effects

Fifteen effects. Switching morphs each card to its new place. Every move is animated from wherever it is, at the speed you let go. holo.create(el, {items, key, render}). set, append and remove do a keyed update - slots are reused, not rebuilt. Switch the effect; loop: 'infinite' wraps seamlessly.

gestures: swipe, tap, double-tap or long-press a card

Loading: placeholders while items arrive

items can be a promise: skeletons stand in until it settles, then the items fade up in turn. Images show a placeholder until they arrive (placeholder: 'shimmer' | 'pulse' | 'blur' | 'none').

Presenting

Presentation transitions (cover, reveal, wipe, iris, kenburns), a countdown bar (progress), and [data-build] content that comes in step by step (builds). Space or Enter goes on; try full screen.

Linked: holo.link(main, thumbs, 'follow')

The thumbnails follow the main view and keep its item in sight; clicking a thumbnail moves the main view. The position is kept in the URL (hash: 'photo').

Nested: carousels inside a carousel

Each shelf is a sub-branch of the outer carousel (holo/shelves/shelf-0). Drag a shelf to its end and keep going - the outer one takes over. Arrow keys only move the carousel you are focused on.

One tile and no controls below 700px, three with controls above. Its channels register only when it comes near the screen (defer: true).
