Tabs preset

Markup becomes a tablist; arrow keys move between panels. One tab lists API-driven skills, another mirrors pinned gallery items from shared state.

Motion

Gestures & effects

Holo runs every move through one animator: drags land at release speed, trackpad momentum settles on the nearest item, and switching effect morphs cards in place (fifteen built-ins, from slide to Ken Burns).

Parent and child carousels negotiate the same pointer — drag a nested strip to its end and the pager takes over on that axis.

Cyre

Channels & bindings

Carousels are Cyre channels. holo.state holds app data; bindInput, bindAll, and list keep the DOM in sync without manual listeners.
